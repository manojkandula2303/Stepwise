=== d-data-comparison | Data Comparison
summary: Comparing two entities across years: profit, margins, growth and ratios.
---
Comparison sets ask you to judge **two or more entities** on derived measures. Build the derived columns first.

## Derived measures

- **Profit** = revenue − expense
- **Profit margin** = profit ÷ revenue
- **Expense ratio** = expense ÷ revenue (lower is better)
- **Growth** = (later − earlier) ÷ earlier

:::tip
A company can have the higher **profit** but the lower **margin**. Read whether the question asks about the absolute amount or the ratio.
:::

:::context
**Revenue and expenses (₹ crore)**

| Year | A revenue | A expense | B revenue | B expense |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 100 | 80 | 120 | 100 |
| 2022 | 120 | 90 | 130 | 110 |
| 2023 | 150 | 105 | 140 | 112 |
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What was A's profit in 2023?
- ₹40 crore
- [x] ₹45 crore
- ₹50 crore
- ₹35 crore
> 150 − 105.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: Which company made more profit in 2022?
- B
- [x] A
- Both made the same
- Cannot be determined
> A made 30 and B made 20.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What was A's profit margin in 2023?
- 25%
- [x] 30%
- 35%
- 45%
> 45/150.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: In which year did both companies make the same profit?
- 2022
- [x] 2021
- 2023
- Never
> Both made 20 in 2021.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: By what percent did B's revenue grow from 2021 to 2023?
- 20%
- [x] 16.67%
- 14.29%
- 12%
> 20/120.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What was B's total profit over the three years?
- ₹60 crore
- [x] ₹68 crore
- ₹72 crore
- ₹78 crore
> 20 + 20 + 28.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is the ratio of A's total revenue to B's total revenue over the three years?
- 39 : 37
- [x] 37 : 39
- 15 : 14
- 1 : 1
> 370 : 390.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: By what percent did A's expense grow from 2021 to 2023?
- 25%
- [x] 31.25%
- 30%
- 35%
> 25/80.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: Which company had the higher expense-to-revenue ratio in 2023?
- A
- [x] B
- Both had the same
- Cannot be determined
> B's was 0.8 and A's was 0.7.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What was A's average annual profit over the three years (approximately)?
- ₹30 crore
- [x] ₹31.67 crore
- ₹35 crore
- ₹28.33 crore
> (20 + 30 + 45)/3.
:::

=== d-percentage-di | Percentage-Based DI
summary: Pass rates, shares of failures and percentage comparisons computed from raw counts.
---
Many DI sets come down to **percentage of** and **percentage more or less than**.

- Pass % = passed ÷ appeared × 100
- "X is what % more than Y" = (X − Y) ÷ **Y** × 100 (the base is the thing after "than")
- Share of a total = part ÷ total × 100

:::warning
"Overall pass %" is **total passed ÷ total appeared**, not the average of the schools' percentages.
:::

:::context
**Exam results**

| School | Appeared | Passed |
|---|---|---|
| P | 400 | 340 |
| Q | 500 | 400 |
| R | 300 | 270 |
| S | 600 | 450 |
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What is school Q's pass percentage?
- 75%
- [x] 80%
- 85%
- 90%
> 400/500.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: Which school has the highest pass percentage?
- P
- Q
- [x] R
- S
> R 90%, P 85%, Q 80%, S 75%.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is the overall pass percentage (approximately)?
- 82.5%
- [x] 81.11%
- 80%
- 83.33%
> 1460/1800.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: How many students failed at school S?
- 100
- [x] 150
- 200
- 120
> 600 − 450.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: Approximately what percentage of all failures came from school S?
- 40%
- [x] 44.1%
- 50%
- 37.5%
> 340 failed in total, and 150/340 ≈ 44.1%.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: By what percent does P's number of passes exceed R's?
- 20.6%
- [x] 25.93%
- 30%
- 70%
> (340 − 270)/270.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: Next year Q's pass rate rises to 90% with the same number appearing. How many extra students pass?
- 40
- [x] 50
- 60
- 45
> 450 − 400.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: Which school has the lowest pass percentage?
- Q
- [x] S
- P
- R
> S has 75%.
:::

:::context
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: x is 25% more than y. By what percent is y less than x?
- 25%
- [x] 20%
- 22.5%
- 15%
> 25/125.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: A value rises 20% and then a further 25%. What is the overall increase?
- 45%
- [x] 50%
- 48%
- 55%
> 1.2 × 1.25 = 1.5.
:::

=== d-ratio-di | Ratio-Based DI
summary: Simplifying ratios from tables, comparing groups and adjusting ratios when values change.
---
Ratio questions test whether you can **simplify** cleanly and **update** a ratio when the numbers change.

- Simplify by dividing both terms by their HCF: 36 : 24 = 3 : 2.
- To compare ratios, convert them to fractions or decimals.
- To combine groups, add the actual values first, never the ratios.

:::context
**Students by class**

| Class | Boys | Girls |
|---|---|---|
| VI | 30 | 20 |
| VII | 36 | 24 |
| VIII | 25 | 35 |
| IX | 40 | 32 |
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What is the ratio of boys to girls in Class VI?
- 2 : 3
- [x] 3 : 2
- 5 : 3
- 3 : 1
> 30 : 20.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: Which other class has the same boys-to-girls ratio as Class VI?
- VIII
- [x] VII
- IX
- None
> 36 : 24 = 3 : 2.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: Which class has more girls than boys?
- VI
- VII
- [x] VIII
- IX
> 35 girls and 25 boys.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is the ratio of total boys to total girls?
- 111 : 131
- [x] 131 : 111
- 6 : 5
- 4 : 3
> 131 boys and 111 girls.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What is the ratio of boys to girls in Class IX?
- 4 : 5
- [x] 5 : 4
- 8 : 7
- 10 : 9
> 40 : 32.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What is the ratio of girls in Class VIII to girls in Class IX?
- 32 : 35
- [x] 35 : 32
- 7 : 8
- 5 : 4
> 35 : 32 (already in its simplest form).
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: If 5 more girls join Class VI, what is its new boys-to-girls ratio?
- 5 : 6
- [x] 6 : 5
- 3 : 2
- 1 : 1
> 30 : 25.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What is the ratio of total students in Class VII to Class IX?
- 6 : 5
- [x] 5 : 6
- 3 : 4
- 4 : 5
> 60 : 72.
:::

:::context
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: If A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5, what is A : C?
- 2 : 5
- [x] 8 : 15
- 6 : 5
- 10 : 12
> 8 : 12 : 15.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: Boys and girls are in the ratio 7 : 5, and there are 24 more boys than girls. How many students are there?
- 120
- [x] 144
- 168
- 96
> 2 parts = 24, so 1 part = 12 and 12 parts = 144.
:::

=== d-average-di | Average-Based DI
summary: Means across periods, required values to reach a target average, medians and corrections.
---
- **Average** = sum ÷ count. Compute the sum once and reuse it.
- **Required value** for a target average = target × new count − current sum.
- **Median** = the middle value of the sorted data (the mean of the two middle values when the count is even).
- **Correcting an error:** adjust the sum by (correct − wrong), then divide again.

:::context
**Monthly sales of a shop (units)**

| Month | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | May | Jun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | 120 | 150 | 130 | 170 | 160 | 190 |
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What were the average monthly sales (approximately)?
- 150
- [x] 153.33
- 155
- 160
> 920/6.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What were the average sales for January to March (approximately)?
- 130
- [x] 133.33
- 135
- 140
> 400/3.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: In how many months were sales above the six-month average?
- 2
- [x] 3
- 4
- 1
> April, May and June.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What were the average sales for April to June (approximately)?
- 170
- [x] 173.33
- 175
- 180
> 520/3.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What must July's sales be for the seven-month average to reach 160?
- 180
- [x] 200
- 190
- 210
> 7 × 160 − 920 = 200.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: By how much does the April–June average exceed the January–March average?
- 30
- [x] 40
- 35
- 45
> 173.33 − 133.33.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: If February's sales had been 30 higher, what would the six-month average be (approximately)?
- 155
- [x] 158.33
- 160
- 153.33
> 950/6.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is the median monthly sales figure?
- 150
- [x] 155
- 160
- 153.33
> Sorted: 120, 130, 150, 160, 170, 190. The median is (150 + 160)/2.
:::

:::context
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: Ten numbers average 25. One of them, 34, was misread as 43. What is the correct average?
- 25.9
- [x] 24.1
- 24.5
- 25
> The sum falls by 9, from 250 to 241.
:::

:::mcq hard
Q: Twenty staff average ₹30,000 in salary. Adding the manager raises the average by ₹2,000. What is the manager's salary?
- ₹62,000
- [x] ₹72,000
- ₹32,000
- ₹70,000
> 21 × 32,000 − 20 × 30,000 = 72,000.

=== d-graph-calculations | Growth and Graph Calculations
summary: CAGR, index numbers, percentage points vs percent, the rule of 72 and share changes.
---
## Growth tools

- **Simple growth:** (end − start) ÷ start
- **CAGR** (compound annual growth): (end ÷ start)^(1/n) − 1
  - 200 → 242 in 2 years: √1.21 = 1.1, so **10%** a year
- **Rule of 72:** years to double ≈ 72 ÷ growth rate in %. At 8% a year, about **9 years**.
- **Index numbers:** with the base year = 100, an index of 135 means **+35%** since the base.

## Percentage points vs percent

A rate going from 10% to 12% is a rise of **2 percentage points**, which is a **20%** increase in the rate itself.

## Shares vs values

A segment's share can fall while its value rises, if the total grows faster. Always check which one the question asks about.

:::mcq medium
Q: A value grows from 200 to 242 in 2 years. What is the compound annual growth rate?
- 21%
- [x] 10%
- 10.5%
- 11%
> (242/200)^(1/2) = 1.1.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: With 2015 as the base year (index 100), the 2020 index is 135. By what percent did the value grow from 2015 to 2020?
- 13.5%
- [x] 35%
- 135%
- 3.5%
> The index rose from 100 to 135.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: A's share rises from 20% to 25% while the total rises from 500 to 600. By what percent does A's value grow?
- 25%
- [x] 50%
- 20%
- 5%
> A goes from 100 to 150.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: A value doubles in 3 years. What is the approximate annual growth rate?
- 33%
- [x] 26%
- 30%
- 20%
> ∛2 ≈ 1.26.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: Using the rule of 72, roughly how long does an investment take to double at 8% a year?
- 8 years
- [x] 9 years
- 12 years
- 6 years
> 72/8 = 9.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: A's sales grew from 400 to 500 and B's from 300 to 390. Who grew faster in percentage terms?
- A
- [x] B
- They grew equally
- Cannot be determined
> A grew 25% and B grew 30%.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: An interest rate rises from 10% to 12%. By what percent did the rate itself increase?
- 2%
- [x] 20%
- 12%
- 10%
> It rose 2 percentage points, and 2/10 = 20%.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: A value of 150 in 2021 grows 20% in 2022 and then falls 10% in 2023. What is the 2023 value?
- 165
- [x] 162
- 160
- 168
> 150 × 1.2 × 0.9.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: X's value was 120 out of a total of 400 in 2022, and 150 out of 600 in 2023. How did X's share change?
- It rose by 5 percentage points
- [x] It fell by 5 percentage points
- It stayed the same
- It rose by 25%
> The share went from 30% to 25%.
:::

:::mcq hard
Q: A value grows from 1000 to 1331 in 3 years. What is the CAGR?
- 11.03%
- [x] 10%
- 33.1%
- 9%
> ∛1.331 = 1.1.
:::

=== d-approximation-di | Approximation in DI
summary: Rounding to friendly numbers to estimate fast when the options are far apart.
---
When the options are well separated, **estimate**. Round each figure to a convenient value and keep track of whether you rounded up or down.

## Friendly anchors

- 49.8% ≈ 50% (one half), 33.3% ≈ 1/3, 12.5% = 1/8, 16.67% = 1/6
- 39.9 ≈ 40, 601 ≈ 600, 9.98² ≈ 100
- For products, if one factor is rounded up and the other down, the errors partly cancel.

:::tip
If two options are within about 2% of each other, approximation isn't safe. Compute exactly instead.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: About what is 49.8% of 601?
- 250
- [x] 300
- 320
- 350
> About half of 600.
:::

:::mcq easy

- [x] 50

- 45
> 2000/40.
:::

:::mcq easy

- [x] 140

> 40 + 100.
:::

:::mcq easy

> 25 × 16.

:::mcq easy
Q: About what is 33.3% of 899?

=== d-data-sufficiency | Data Sufficiency (Quant)
summary: Deciding whether the statements give enough data for a unique numeric answer.
---
The options are the same as in the reasoning version:

- **A:** I alone is sufficient.
- **B:** II alone is sufficient.
- **C:** Either alone is sufficient.
- **D:** Both are needed together.
- **E:** Not sufficient even together.

## Quant-specific checks

- Count **unknowns vs independent equations**. Two unknowns need two independent equations.
- Watch for **squares**: x² = 9 allows ±3, but x³ = 27 gives exactly 3.
- For yes/no questions, a statement is sufficient if it always gives the **same** answer, even if that answer is "no".
- Don't solve fully. Stop once you know whether a unique answer exists.

:::mcq medium
Q: What is x? I. x + y = 10. II. x − y = 2.
- I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- [x] Both together
> Two equations are needed for two unknowns.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: Is x > 0? I. x² = 9. II. x³ = 27.
- I alone
- [x] II alone
- Either alone
- Both together
> II gives x = 3. I allows x = −3.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is the area of a rectangle? I. Its perimeter is 20. II. Its length is 6.
- I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- [x] Both together
> Together they give a breadth of 4, so the area is 24.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is x? I. x is a prime number. II. x < 10.
- I alone
- II alone
- Both together
- [x] Not sufficient even together
> x could be 2, 3, 5 or 7.
:::

:::mcq hard
Q: What is the train's speed? I. It crosses a 200 m platform in 20 s. II. It crosses a pole in 10 s.
- I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- [x] Both together
> L = 10v and L + 200 = 20v give v = 20 m/s.
:::

:::mcq hard
Q: Is n divisible by 6? I. n is divisible by 3. II. n is divisible by 4.
- I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- [x] Both together
> Together n is a multiple of 12. Alone, each could go either way (9 or 6; 4 or 12).
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is the radius of a circle? I. Its area is 154 cm². II. Its circumference is 44 cm.
- I alone
- II alone
- [x] Either alone
- Both together
> Each statement alone gives r = 7.
:::

:::mcq easy
Q: What is the average of five numbers? I. Their sum is 100. II. The largest is 30.
- [x] I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- Both together
> 100/5 = 20.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: What is A's share of the profit? I. A and B invested in the ratio 2 : 3. II. The total profit is ₹5000.
- I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- [x] Both together
> You need both the ratio and the total.
:::

:::mcq medium
Q: How many students passed? I. 80% of the students passed. II. 40 students failed.
- I alone
- II alone
- Either alone
- [x] Both together
> 20% = 40, so the total is 200 and 160 passed.
:::
